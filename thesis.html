PhD Thesis

I defended my thesis titled

Attaining Basically Everything in Attribute-Based Encryption: Simplifying the Design of Practical Schemes via Pair Encodings

at Radboud University Nijmegen on Tuesday 19 September 2023, with the defense starting at 14:30 (CEST). A recording of the defense exists — feel free to email me if you'd like to see it.

My thesis looks at how attribute-based encryption (ABE) can enforce fine-grained access control in practice, using the pair encodings framework to simplify the design, implementation and cryptanalysis of ABE schemes. It also introduces two new schemes, GLUE and TinyABE, that offer flexible efficiency trade-offs, along with a generic transformation for chosen-ciphertext security.
